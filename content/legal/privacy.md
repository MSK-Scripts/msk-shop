## 2. Overview of Data Processing

### Data We Collect

When using this website and shop, the following personal data may be processed:

- **CFX.re / FiveM username and user ID** — required to authenticate your account and deliver purchased resources via the FiveM Asset Escrow system
- **Discord ID** — required for packages that include Discord role assignment
- **IP address** — collected automatically when creating a shopping basket; transmitted to Tebex for fraud prevention
- **Shopping basket data** — stored locally in your browser (localStorage) to persist your cart between sessions
- **Technical log data** — our web server automatically records access logs including IP address, browser type, date and time of access

### Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect or process payment data. All payment processing is handled exclusively by **Tebex Limited**.
- We do not use tracking cookies, analytics services, or advertising technologies.
- We do not create user accounts or store personal profiles on our servers.

## 4. Cookies and Local Storage

### Local Storage (Shopping Cart)

We use your browser's **localStorage** to save your shopping cart (basket identifier). This is a technical necessity to provide the shopping cart function.

- **What is stored:** A unique basket identifier assigned by Tebex
- **Where it is stored:** Exclusively in your browser (not on our servers)
- **Duration:** Until you clear your browser data or the basket expires
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — necessary for the service

### Session Storage

We use **sessionStorage** to temporarily store authentication state during the login flow (FiveM and Discord OAuth). This data is automatically deleted when you close your browser tab.

### Cookies

This website does **not** use cookies for tracking, analytics, or advertising purposes. No cookie consent banner is required as no non-essential cookies are set.

## 6. Authentication via CFX.re / FiveM

To purchase packages, you must authenticate with your **CFX.re account** via the Tebex identity service (`ident.tebex.io`). During this process:

- You are redirected to Tebex's identity service
- Tebex verifies your FiveM identity and links it to your basket
- Your FiveM username and user ID are returned to our shop to complete the purchase

We store your FiveM username temporarily in your browser's localStorage for the duration of your session.

---

## 7. Authentication via Discord

Certain packages require linking your **Discord account**. During this process:

- You are redirected to Tebex's identity service for Discord OAuth
- Tebex verifies your Discord identity
- Your Discord ID is returned to our shop and passed to Tebex as part of the package purchase

We do not permanently store your Discord ID on our servers. It is only held temporarily in your browser's sessionStorage during the purchase flow.

## 8. Web Server Logs

Our hosting provider automatically records access logs containing:
- IP address
- Date and time of access
- URL requested
- HTTP status code
- Browser/client type (User-Agent)

These logs are used for security and operational purposes and are retained for a maximum of **14 days**, after which they are automatically deleted.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest in operating and securing the website.

---

## 9. Discord Online Count

Our homepage displays the current number of online members in our Discord server. To retrieve this information, our server queries the **Discord API** (`discord.com/api`) when a visitor loads the page. The result is cached for 60 seconds on our server, so individual visitors do not cause repeated requests to Discord.

**No personal data** is transmitted to Discord as part of this request.
