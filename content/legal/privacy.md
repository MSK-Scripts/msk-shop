## 2. Payment Processing via Tebex

All purchases are processed by **Tebex Limited**. The following data is transmitted to Tebex to complete your purchase:

- CFX.re / FiveM username and user ID
- Discord ID (for packages requiring Discord authentication)
- IP address
- Payment data (processed exclusively by Tebex)

Tebex processes payment data in accordance with their own privacy policy: [checkout.tebex.io/privacy](https://checkout.tebex.io/privacy)

## 3. Discord Authentication

Certain packages require Discord login. Only your Discord ID is processed for identification purposes. We do not permanently store any additional Discord profile information beyond what is necessary for delivery.

## 4. CFX.re / FiveM Authentication

A CFX.re account login is required to complete purchases. Your FiveM username and user ID are processed to deliver purchased resources via the FiveM Asset Escrow system.

## 5. Local Storage

We use browser local storage to temporarily save your shopping cart (basket ID). No tracking cookies or marketing cookies are used.
